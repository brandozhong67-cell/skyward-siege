# Skyward Siege

A little lane defense game. Build a team of skyward defenders and protect the beacon from waves of cloud enemies.

**Play:** [Skyward Siege on GitHub Pages](https://brandozhong67-cell.github.io/skyward-siege/skyward-siege.html)

## Goal

Stop enemies from reaching the beacon. If too many get through, the beacon loses health. Keep it standing through increasingly difficult waves.

## How to play

1. Choose a map and difficulty, then select **Start defending**.
2. Select a defender from the right-hand list.
3. Click an open patch beside the trail to place it. Place defenders near bends so they can attack enemies for longer.
4. Place at least one defender, then start the wave. Defenders automatically attack enemies in range.
5. Earn coins and XP as you fight. Spend coins on more defenders, upgrades, and power-ups; earn XP to unlock more defenders.
6. Between or during waves, pause to plan, change game speed, or adjust a selected defender.

## Placement and tower controls

Defenders must be placed on open ground beside the trail: not on the trail, not too far away to reach enemies, and with space between other defenders. On **Cinder Volcano**, avoid the glowing magma vents; eruptions can damage nearby units.

Click a placed defender to select it. You can upgrade it, sell it, or change its targeting priority among **First**, **Strongest**, and **Closest**. Each defender can be upgraded up to three times. Hover over an open patch to preview the defender's attack range; red indicates an invalid spot.

## Waves and difficulty

The first wave has 10 enemies, and each later wave adds three more. Enemies grow tougher as waves advance. Every fifth wave is a boss wave; after wave 50, Tower Breakers begin attacking nearby defenders. The game starts with 240 coins. Starting beacon health is 140 on Easy, 100 on Normal, and 75 on Hard; harder settings also make enemies tougher and faster.

## Defenders

The first five defenders are available from the start:

- **Lookout** — quick, all-round shots
- **Frostbell** — slows nearby enemies
- **Starcaller** — heavy hit with a slower reload
- **Sunlance** — long range and heavy damage
- **Emberpot** — splash damage for groups

More defenders unlock with XP. The roster adds different strengths, including rapid fire, slowing, long-range attacks, splash damage, and attacks that arc between enemies. Try different combinations to cover the whole trail.

## Power-ups

Power-ups cost coins. Some are one-use effects, such as a repair, coin cache, freeze, or blast. Others last for several waves and improve range, damage, firing speed, protection, or repairs. Some power-ups unlock only after reaching a later wave. Their costs and effects are listed in the game.

## Maps

- **Cloud Harbor** — winding harbor trail
- **Ember Ridge** — rocky ridge route
- **Moonlit Marsh** — winding marsh trail
- **Cinder Volcano** — volcanic route with erupting vents

## Progress and developer mode

XP and unlocked defenders are saved in this browser. The **DEV** menu contains testing controls, including unlimited resources, automatic waves, extra speeds, and jumping to a chosen wave.
